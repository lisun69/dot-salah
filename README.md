# Dot Salah

A free 5-times prayer (Salah) tracker for Android with a resizable home-screen widget.
Black, red and white dot-matrix design. Prayer times for Bangladesh (Dhaka by default).

## Download
Open **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** on your Android phone and download **DotSalah.apk** (Android 8 or newer).
Allow "Install unknown apps" when asked. If Play Protect warns you, tap **More details > Install anyway**.

## Features
- **Widget**: location and next prayer with live countdown on the left; today's Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha times on the right. Tap a row to tick it (red tick). Two dot bars: today (each prayer = 20%) and this month (days with all 5).
- **Reminders**: notification with vibration at every prayer time, with a "Mark as prayed" button.
- **Month**: dot calendar (white = all 5 prayed), daily chart, consistency %, streak, and per-prayer consistency. Tap a past day to edit.
- **Setup**: 12/24 hour time, 12 Bangladesh cities, Hanafi/Standard Asr, minute adjustments, reminder on/off, vibration on/off.

## Prayer times
Calculated on the phone (no internet) using the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi method with Hanafi Asr.
Default minute adjustments match the Islamic Foundation Bangladesh timetable. Always follow your local mosque.

## Privacy
No account, no internet, no ads. Your prayer record stays on your phone.
