# Dot Salah

A free 5-times prayer (Salah) tracker for Android with a resizable home-screen widget.
Dot-matrix design. Prayer times for Bangladesh (Dhaka by default).

## Download
Open **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** on your Android phone and download **DotSalah.apk** (Android 8 or newer).
Allow "Install unknown apps" when asked. If Play Protect warns you, tap **More details > Install anyway**.
Already installed? Just install the new APK over it. Your ticks and settings are kept.

## Features
- **Widget**: location and next prayer with live countdown on the left; today's 5 times on the right. Tap a row to tick it. Two dot bars: today (20% per prayer) and this month.
- **Widget themes (new in 1.1)**: AMOLED, Light and Clean, with adjustable background opacity. Tap the small dot-gear on the widget.
- **Ramadan (new in 1.1)**: Sehri and Iftar times with countdown and a full Ramadan timetable. Appears automatically during Ramadan.
- **Reminders**: notification with vibration at every prayer time, with a "Mark as prayed" button.
- **Month**: dot calendar, daily chart, consistency %, streak and per-prayer consistency.
- **Setup**: 12/24 hour, 12 Bangladesh cities, Hanafi/Standard Asr, minute adjustments, Hijri date adjustment.

## Prayer times
Calculated on the phone (no internet) using the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi method with Hanafi Asr.
Default adjustments match the Islamic Foundation Bangladesh timetable. Always follow your local mosque.

## Privacy
No account, no internet, no ads. Your prayer record stays on your phone.
